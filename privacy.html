Privacy Policy.

Your information. Your control. Here's exactly how it works.

Last updated: February 2026

I keep this simple because I mean it: your data is yours. Here's exactly what I collect, why, and what I do with it.

Who I am

soffyn is a product of Developed By Akia, LLC. If you have questions about this policy, email me directly at hello@soffyn.com.

What I collect

When you join the soffyn waitlist, I collect your first name and email address. That's it. I don't collect your location, phone number, or any other personal information unless you choose to share it with me directly.

Why I collect it

I use your email address to send you updates about soffyn's launch and to invite you to download the app as a founding member when soffyn enters beta. I will never use your email for anything else without your permission.

Who I share it with

I use MailerLite to manage the waitlist. Your information is stored securely in MailerLite. I don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else. Ever.

How long I keep it

I keep your email on the waitlist until you unsubscribe. Every email I send includes an unsubscribe link. One click and you're off the list — no questions asked.

Your rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your information at any time. Just email me at hello@soffyn.com and I'll take care of it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If I ever change this policy I'll update the date at the top and let the waitlist know. I won't make changes that compromise your privacy without telling you first.

Questions?

Email me at hello@soffyn.com. I'm a real person and I actually respond.
