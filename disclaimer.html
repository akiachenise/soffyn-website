Wellness Disclaimer.

Last updated: September 27, 2026 | Developed By Akia, LLC

1. About soffyn

soffyn is a wellness app in development, intended to support general well-being. This website shares information about the app and provides a waitlist.

2. Limits of wellness support

soffyn is not a medical or mental health service, a diagnostic tool, or a crisis intervention service. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or mental health condition.

Wellness content on this website is for general information, not medical advice. It does not replace care from a qualified healthcare professional. For questions about your health or treatment, speak with your healthcare provider.

3. Crisis and emergency support

If you are in the United States and need urgent support:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988, or visit 988lifeline.org. Available 24/7.
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741. Visit Crisis Text Line for more information. Available 24/7.
  • Immediate danger or a medical emergency: Call 911.

Outside the United States, contact a local crisis service or your local emergency number.

4. General mental health information and support

In the United States, the NAMI HelpLine offers mental health information, resources, and support. Call 1-800-950-6264, Monday–Friday, 10 a.m.–10 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding federal holidays. The HelpLine is not a crisis service.

5. Acknowledgment

By using soffyn.com, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer and agree that soffyn is not a substitute for professional mental health care.
