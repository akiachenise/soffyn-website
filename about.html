I'm building the app I couldn't find.

I'm Akia.

I'm a neurodivergent Black woman, software engineer, and accessibility specialist.

The wellness apps I tried felt too generic, too demanding, or just too much for my brain. I'm a forgetful girl. Out of sight, out of mind. Streaks don't work for me. My hopes and dreams have a habit of becoming hoarded thoughts in the notes app.

After spending all day masking — at work, in every room — I didn't want to come home to something that asked me to perform even more.

I needed a space that meets me in the middle. Somewhere I could put a thought down, check in with myself, and come back after time away without feeling behind.

That's why I'm building soffyn for neurodivergent Black women.

soffyn is in development. Join the waitlist for updates as I build it and news about opportunities to try it.
