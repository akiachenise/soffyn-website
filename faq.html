About soffyn

soffyn is a wellness app in development for neurodivergent Black women. It’s being built with journaling, affirmations, mood check-ins, flexible goals, and a focus timer. No streaks. No shame.

soffyn centers neurodivergent Black women. If its approach feels useful to you, you’re welcome here regardless of who you are.

No. You don’t need a formal diagnosis to use soffyn.

A launch date hasn’t been announced yet. Join the waitlist and I’ll let you know when soffyn is ready.

soffyn is being built as a web app first, for use in a browser. iOS and Android apps are planned for later; release dates haven’t been announced.

soffyn is being designed around flexible use: choose the tools that help you and return when you need them. There are no streaks to maintain or guilt-based reminders for time away.

I’m Akia, a neurodivergent Black woman, software engineer, and accessibility specialist. Read the story behind soffyn →
