About soffyn

soffyn is a wellness app I built for neurodivergent Black women. Daily affirmations, flexible journaling, goal tracking, a focus timer, quick-capture, and privacy controls. No streaks. No shame. Just a space that meets you where you are.

soffyn is built specifically for neurodivergent Black women. If you've been looking for a wellness space that doesn't feel like it was built for someone else's brain, you're welcome here regardless of who you are.

soffyn launches as a web app in September 2026. iOS and Android apps follow in early 2027. Join the waitlist to get in first.

Calm and Headspace are built for neurotypical users. They assume you'll show up consistently, meditate on a schedule, and maintain streaks. soffyn is built for brains that don't work that way. No streaks that punish you for being human. No overwhelming menus. No toxic positivity. Just tools that actually get how your brain works.

I'm Akia — a neurodivergent Black woman, developer, and accessibility consultant. I built soffyn because I couldn't find a wellness app built for a brain like mine. soffyn is a product of Developed By Akia, LLC.

No. soffyn is for anyone who recognizes themselves in what we're describing — whether you have a diagnosis, are pursuing one, or just know your brain has always worked differently. You don't need a piece of paper to belong here.

soffyn is built for adults and teens 13 and up. The content isn't adult — but it isn't childlike either. It's built for anyone old enough to have started feeling the weight of masking and performing, and ready for somewhere they don't have to.
