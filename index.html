New here? A quick note.

What does "neurodivergent" mean?

Neurodivergent describes brains that think, learn, and experience the world differently — including ADHD, autism, dyslexia, anxiety, OCD, and more. It's not a flaw. It's just how your brain works.

If you've spent your life in wellness spaces that weren't built for how your brain actually works — you're in the right place. You don't have to explain yourself here.

About soffyn

I built the app I couldn't find.

I built this because I needed it and it didn't exist.

I'm a neurodivergent Black woman, software engineer, and accessibility specialist. I've tried almost every app. They were either too cookie cutter, too demanding, or just too much for my brain. I'm a forgetful girl. Out of sight, out of mind. Streaks don't work for me. My hopes and dreams have a habit of becoming hoarded thoughts in the notes app.

And no app understood the specific exhaustion of spending all day masking — at work, in every room — and coming home to something that asked me to perform even more.

I needed something that meets me in the middle. A space that's gentle, rewarding, and completely okay if I don't show up every single day.

Always there when you need it. Never mad when you don't.

That's soffyn. I built it for me. And I built it for you.
