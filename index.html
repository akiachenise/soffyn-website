You hold it together all day. At work. In every room. soffyn is where you don't have to.

A wellness app for neurodivergent Black women—journaling, affirmations, mood check-ins, flexible goals, and a focus timer.

No streaks. No shame.

Journaling, check-ins, and a place to start.

I’m building soffyn so you can write something down, check in with yourself, or spend a few minutes getting started. You choose what’s useful and leave the rest.

Journaling

However the thought comes out.

Leave a few words or write a longer entry. You don’t have to organize the thought before putting it down.

Affirmations

Affirmations you don't have to brace against.

Words that acknowledge a difficult day without asking you to turn it into a positive one.

Mood check-ins

Noticing, not scoring.

Check in with how you feel without rating how well you’re doing.

Flexible goals

Held, not graded.

Keep track of what matters to you, with room for your plans to change.

Focus timer

A soft start, before you talk yourself out of it.

Give one thing your attention for a while, with a timer to help you begin.

Still here when you come back.
