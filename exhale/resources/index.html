Wellness resources

A resource guide to keep

A few places to turn for mental health care, community, ADHD support, and practical help. These resources were gathered for Exhale. and are here whenever you want to return.

Need to talk to someone now? In the U.S., call 988 or text 988 for free, 24/7 crisis and emotional support.

Black women’s wellness & community

Chicago · Mental wellness

Sista Afya Community Care

Culturally centered mental wellness support, including therapy, peer support, groups, and workshops. Insurance, Medicaid, and reduced-fee options are available for some services.

Fees and program eligibility vary.

Explore support options ↗

Chicago · Black mothers

Black Mom Ish / MotherWell Collective

Mental health scholarship opportunities, care connections, and whole-body wellness resources for Black mothers.

Scholarship availability and eligibility vary.

Explore MotherWell ↗

Nationwide · Therapist directory

Therapy for Black Girls

A directory for finding culturally responsive therapists, alongside mental wellness articles and podcast conversations.

Directory access is free; individual providers set fees.

Find a therapist ↗

Nationwide · Therapy assistance

The Loveland Foundation

The Loveland Therapy Fund offers financial assistance for therapy to eligible Black women and nonbinary individuals.

Applications open quarterly; funding and eligibility are limited.

Explore the Therapy Fund ↗

Mental health & recovery services

Chicago & Illinois · Mental health and recovery

Thresholds

Mental health and substance-use services, psychiatric care, and support related to housing and employment.

Sliding-fee options are available based on household income and size.

Explore Thresholds services ↗ Call 773-572-5500

Chicago & suburban Cook County · Helpline

NAMI Chicago

Free, confidential guidance, emotional support, and referrals to local mental health resources.

Helpline hours: Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–8 p.m.; Sat–Sun 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Central.

Learn about the NAMI Chicago Helpline ↗ Call 833-626-4244

Neurodivergent support

Nationwide · Adult ADHD

CHADD

Education for adults with ADHD, resources for managing everyday challenges, and information about local and online support.

Explore adult ADHD resources ↗

Online · Peer community

Neurospicy Black Women

A private Facebook group where Black women can ask questions and share experiences with ADHD, autism, and other forms of neurodivergence. The website lists workshops, events, and community resources.

Visit the Facebook group ↗ Visit the Neurospicy Black Women website ↗

Community & everyday assistance

Cook County · Everyday support

211 Metro Chicago

Free, 24/7 referrals for food, housing, healthcare, utility assistance, and other non-emergency community services.

Text your ZIP code to 898211, or call 211.

Find help through 211 ↗ Call 211

Immediate emotional support

United States · Free 24/7 support

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential support by phone, text, or chat for emotional distress, mental health crises, and suicidal thoughts.

Call or text 988, 24 hours a day.

Get help through 988 ↗ Call 988

These organizations are independent resources. Inclusion here does not indicate a sponsorship, formal partnership, or endorsement. Services, costs, eligibility, and availability can change. Contact each organization for current information.

This guide provides information and does not replace professional medical or mental health care. If there is an immediate threat to life or safety in the U.S., call 911. Outside the U.S., use your local emergency number.
